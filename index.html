About

Hi, I’m Nefe Clarke. I’m a communications and email marketing specialist and an IT project manager, and I’m driven by a passion to communicate clearly and deliver what I promise.

Notes

Short thoughts on the work I do.

Email marketing

Most email problems are listening problems. Before I write a single subject line, I look at who opened, who clicked and who went quiet. Segmenting a list is just listening at scale. That is how my campaigns lifted customer lifetime value by 87%.

Communication and delivery

A project doesn’t fail on the day it is late. It fails the first week nobody says what is blocking it. Clear updates, written down, sent to the right people. That is most of what a 94% on-time record really looks like.

Media and AI

AI can draft in seconds. It can’t decide what is worth saying or who needs to hear it. I use it to move faster on the repetitive parts, and I keep the thinking and the tone human.

Virtual and executive assistance

You take care of the big stuff. I’ll take care of the small stuff. Most of what slows a leader down isn’t strategy. It’s the inbox, the calendar and the follow-ups nobody has time for. Hand those to me, and you get your best hours back for the decisions only you can make.
